# Preliminary licensing and IP boundaries

**Research only; not legal advice. Consult a qualified software/IP attorney before public distribution.**

- Forge publishes code under GNU GPL v3. Incorporating GPL-covered Forge components in a distributed combined application generally brings corresponding-source and license obligations. Do not assume a separate process or different repo automatically avoids those obligations.
- Argentum publishes its own engine under the MIT License. Permission notices must be retained as required, but MIT is substantially less restrictive than GPL for combined distribution.
- Neither software license grants rights to Wizards of the Coast's copyrighted card illustrations, creative text, symbols, trademarks, or third-party crossover IP.
- Forge's community existence is not evidence of a Wizards license or a legal exemption.
- This repo includes no copied Forge engine code, MTG card database, or card images. Manual scripts clone public Forge source into an ignored local `third_party/` directory.
- Do not add code, card packs, datasets, image services, mobile distribution, or online multiplayer hosting without a separate license and legal review.
- A user-supplied deck/scan workflow can alter content distribution responsibilities but does **not** itself establish permission to reproduce protected card content.
